Hail Damage Repair Services

Trusted, Experienced, & Ready When the Storm Hits

When hail hits, time is critical — and so is quality.

With over 30 years of hands-on experience, I partner with body shops, dealerships, and dent companies across the country to provide reliable, high-quality paintless dent repair (PDR) services after major storms.

I’m not here to compete — I’m here to help you maximize your revenue, keep your customers happy, and restore vehicles to pre-storm condition with precision craftsmanship.

I Work With:

Body Shops:

Expand your capacity and keep work flowing without sacrificing quality.

Dealerships:

Get your fleet back to pre-loss condition quickly and easily, and profitably.

Dent Repair shops:

A hail storm can be a disaster or it can be your winning lottery ticket! 

Why Partner With Me?

  •  30+ Years of PDR Experience
  •  Certified, Insured, and Professional
  •  High-Quality, No-Drama Repairs
  •  Sublet Work — You Keep a Percentage
  •  Nationwide, Storm-Driven Availability
